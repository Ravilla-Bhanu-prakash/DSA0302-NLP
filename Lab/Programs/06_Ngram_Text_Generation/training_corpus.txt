Natural language processing is a subfield of artificial intelligence and linguistics.
Artificial intelligence empowers computers to understand human language semantics.
Natural language processing systems analyze text, speech, and dialog interactions.
Computers process natural language to perform sentiment analysis, information retrieval, and machine translation.
Language modeling is a foundational task in modern natural language processing systems.
Language models predict the next word in a sequence using conditional statistical probabilities.
Artificial intelligence is transforming automated systems across every technological domain.
Natural language generation produces fluent text based on learned corpus transitions.
Natural language processing enables intelligent agents to summarize complex articles.
